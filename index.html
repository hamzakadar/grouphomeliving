What daily care looks like

Our customized and assisted living program is built around each resident's routine, with help where it's needed and independence where it isn't.

Personal care

Help with bathing, dressing and grooming, given in a way that keeps each resident's own habits and privacy.

Medication assistance

Trained staff keep medication schedules on time and accurate, and track changes from prescribers.

Small-home living

A few residents per home, so staff know each person well and care plans can actually be personal.

Customized and assisted living placements

We offer 24-hour customized living for adults with physical and/or mental disabilities, in homes set up to be comfortable, calm and safe.

Every resident has a care plan written with them, their family and their case manager, and we update it as needs change.

  • Staff on site 24 hours a day
  • Individual care plans and goals
  • Meals and help with daily routines
  • Medication assistance and health monitoring
  • Help getting to appointments and community activities
  • Regular updates for families and case managers

How a referral works

Everything starts with one email, and we keep the conversation there.

  1. Email the referral

    Send the client's details, support plan, MnCHOICES assessment and any other documents that show their needs to referrals@grouphomeliving.com.

  2. We review it

    We check needs, waiver and location against our current openings.

  3. We reply by email

    We confirm fit and next steps, and set up a visit if there's an opening.

  4. Move-in

    We work with the case manager and family on paperwork and a move-in date.
